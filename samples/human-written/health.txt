My Experience with Chronic Back Pain

Got my first back spasm at 28. Just bent down to tie my shoe and couldn't stand back up. Spent the next three days lying on my floor wondering if this was my life now.

Doctor said it was muscular, nothing structural. Gave me some muscle relaxers and told me to take it easy. That was useless advice—I had a job, I couldn't just lie around.

Physical therapy helped some. The exercises were boring but they worked. Cat-cow, bird-dog, some other animal-named stretches. Did them every morning for like six months. Still do some of them when I remember.

What actually fixed it, or at least made it manageable, was changing how I sit. I worked from home even before the pandemic, spent eight hours in a dining chair. Bought a proper office chair and a standing desk converter. Game changer. Also started walking more. Nothing intense, just 20 minutes at lunch.

Still get flare-ups maybe twice a year. Usually when I've been stressed and tense without noticing. There's this spot on my right side that acts up when I'm clenching my jaw while working. Weird how it's all connected.

Sleeping position matters too. Stomach sleeping is apparently terrible for your back. Took me months to train myself to sleep on my side. Used a body pillow. Looked ridiculous but worked.
